This sample has no `cover:` in its settings, so the blog draws one: a sphere of dots, like the one on [v4.run](https://v4.run), tilted and coloured differently for each post. The same post always gets the same picture.

That keeps the homepage looking full even when a post has no photo — useful for quick notes and essays.

Delete this post once there are real ones.
