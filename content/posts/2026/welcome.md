This is the writing corner of [v4.run](https://v4.run). The main site is a playground — a spinning sphere, a red pill and a blue one. This side is quieter on purpose: dark or light, large type, nothing moving while you read.

## What you'll find here

Mostly three kinds of writing:

- **Essays** — longer arguments about access to justice, courts, and how public systems change (or don't).
- **Field notes** — things seen and heard in courtrooms, district offices and on the road.
- **Notes** — short, half-formed thoughts, posted before they're polished.

## How to follow along

There's an [RSS feed](/rss.xml) for feed readers, and every post has a comments section at the bottom. Comments are read before they appear, so it can take a little while.

Thanks for reading.
