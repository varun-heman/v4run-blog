## Text

Body text is set in a serif at about 19 pixels, with roughly 65–70 characters per line — the range that's easiest to read for long stretches. **Bold** stands out, *italics* lean, and `inline code` looks like this. Here's [an internal link](/archive/) and [an external one](https://v4.run).

A second paragraph, to show the spacing between them. Long words like *interdisciplinarity* and long links wrap rather than spilling off the edge of a phone screen.
