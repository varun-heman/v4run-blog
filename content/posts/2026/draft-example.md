---
title: A draft you can only see while previewing
date: 2026-10-26
category: notes
tags: [meta]
draft: true
---

Posts with `draft: true` show up while you preview on your computer, marked with a yellow "Draft" label. They're left out of the live site, the RSS feed and search.

Remove the `draft: true` line (or set it to `false`) when it's ready.
